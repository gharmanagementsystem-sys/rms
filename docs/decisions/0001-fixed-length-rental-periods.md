# ADR 0001 — Rental periods are fixed-length, not calendar months

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Context

`CLAUDE.md` is the engineering source of truth for this project. Section 46 of
that document instructs:

> Do not calculate a monthly period by adding `30 * 24 * 60 * 60`.
> Use calendar-aware date operations.

Section 13 reinforces this by illustrating periods as calendar months
(`2026-08-01 → 2026-08-31`, then `2026-09-01 → 2026-09-30`).

## Decision

The project owner has decided the opposite, and that decision governs:

- A **monthly** rental period is exactly **30 days** from the rental start date.
- A **yearly** rental period is exactly **365 days** from the rental start date.
- Period boundaries are stated **explicitly** (inclusive start, inclusive end)
  so off-by-one errors cannot creep in.

These lengths live in exactly one place: `MONTHLY_PERIOD_DAYS` and
`YEARLY_PERIOD_DAYS` in `packages/shared/src/constants.ts`.

## Consequences

**Benefits.** Month-end, February and leap-year ambiguity disappear entirely.
There is no anchor-day clamping rule, so the classic recurring-billing bug where
a 31st-of-the-month rental silently drifts to the 28th after passing February
cannot occur. Period arithmetic becomes trivially testable.

**Costs.** Billing drifts relative to the calendar. A rental starting 1 August
bills 1–30 August, then 31 August – 29 September, and so on. Because
365 / 30 ≈ 12.17, a tenant pays roughly **12.17 periods of rent per year rather
than 12** — about a **1.4% annual increase** compared with calendar-month
billing — and the due date walks backwards through the month.

This also sits awkwardly with the decision to present dates in Bikram Sambat: a
Nepali landlord who thinks of rent as due _गते_ (on a fixed day of the BS month)
will see the due date move. This should be surfaced clearly in the UI so the
schedule is never a surprise.

**Reversibility.** Because period length is read from two named constants and
generated by a single module, switching to calendar-aware periods later is a
contained change. Historical periods would remain untouched, since every period
snapshots its own boundaries and charges.

## Notes for future contributors

Do not "fix" this to calendar months on the strength of `CLAUDE.md` §46. It is a
deliberate, documented override. If it is ever revisited, it must be revisited
as a product decision with a migration plan for in-flight rentals.
